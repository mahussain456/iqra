Iqra Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026. Applies to the Iqra apps for iPhone and Android and the Iqra website.

The short version

Iqra has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your settings, bookmarks, prayer log and tasbih count stay on your phone. We don't collect, sell or share personal data.

What stays on your device

  • Your city, calculation method and notification choices.
  • Quran bookmarks, your last-read position, the prayers you've ticked off and your tasbih count.

Deleting the app deletes all of this. We never receive a copy.

Location

If you tap "Use my location", Iqra asks your phone for your position once and uses it on your device to work out prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is saved only on your phone and never sent to us or anyone else. You can choose a city instead and never grant location access.

Compass and motion

The Qibla compass reads your phone's compass heading while that screen is open. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Notifications

Prayer and wudu reminders are scheduled locally by your phone. Iqra uses no push server, so no device token or schedule leaves your phone.

Website only: Ask Iqra

The iPhone and Android apps don't include Ask Iqra. On the website, when it is available, a question you type is sent to Anthropic's Claude to write an answer. Don't include personal details in questions.

Fonts

Iqra may load its typefaces from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of a normal web request. See Google's privacy policy for how it handles that.

Children

Iqra is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
